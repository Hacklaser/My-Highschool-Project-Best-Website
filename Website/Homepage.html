Transforming Healthcare Through Ethical AI

This research investigates how artificial intelligence can be implemented responsibly in medical settings to improve patient outcomes while addressing ethical concerns.

About This Research

As of 2025, approximately 66.9% of physicians use AI in their practice (Robeznieks, 2024). While AI has demonstrated remarkable capabilities in diagnosing rare conditions and assisting in complex surgeries, significant challenges remain regarding ethics, privacy, and public trust.

Focus Question

How can the medical and healthcare sector ethically use AI to improve their work?

Research Methodology

This investigation employed a mixed-methods approach:

  • Literature review of industry sources
  • Analysis of current AI implementation statistics
  • Case studies of successful and problematic AI deployments
  • Ethical framework evaluation

The investigation was conducted by Bongani Swelindawo as part of the Computer Applications Technology PAT at Fundulawizi Secondary School.
